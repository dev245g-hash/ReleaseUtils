Useful Freeware Tools

Small, portable Windows utilities. No installer, no registry — unzip and run, settings stay next to the exe. Free for personal and commercial use.

MultiCapture

A portable, tray-based capture & screen-recording tool. One PrintScreen grabs a region, window, monitor or full screen — or starts a recording to MP4. Dashcam mode keeps the last 20–60s running so you can save what already happened.

Download Details

MultiView

A portable multi-view workspace that tiles web pages and local folders into one grid. Type an address and the cell picks its own engine — up to 9 × 9 with row/column spans, and the whole board comes back as you left it.
